## 一、项目创作声明

《寂静边界 Silent Frontier》是一款以科幻文明生存、宇宙博弈和策略构筑为核心的独立游戏项目。

本项目从宇宙社会学、黑暗森林理论、文明生存困境以及信息不对称等科幻思想中汲取创作灵感，尝试将文明之间的猜疑、欺骗、隐蔽、技术发展与生存竞争转化为可交互的游戏机制。

项目在创作理念上受到刘慈欣《三体》系列作品所呈现的部分科幻思想启发，但《寂静边界》拥有独立构建的世界观、原创角色、文明设定、游戏规则、剧情内容、美术设计和系统机制。

## 三、创作理念

《寂静边界》希望探索一个问题：

**当文明无法确认宇宙中其他存在的善意，当暴露自身就可能意味着毁灭，生存究竟需要怎样的智慧？**

游戏并不单纯追求科技增长或军事力量的最大化，而是试图让玩家在有限资源和未知威胁之间作出选择。

发展科技可能带来优势，也可能暴露自身；主动攻击可能消除威胁，也可能引来更强大的敌人；保持沉默能够延续生存，却也可能错失机会。

在这里，玩家需要面对的不只是敌人，还有信息的不完整、判断的风险，以及每一次选择所产生的长期后果。

## 1. Project Overview

*Silent Frontier* is an independent science fiction strategy roguelike game centered on civilization survival, cosmic game theory, strategic deception, and the struggle for existence in an unknown universe.

The project draws creative inspiration from ideas explored in science fiction, including cosmic sociology, the Dark Forest theory, civilization survival dilemmas, and information asymmetry.

These concepts are reinterpreted through original gameplay systems, narrative structures, and interactive experiences.

Although the project is conceptually inspired in part by the philosophical themes of Liu Cixin's *The Three-Body Problem* series, *Silent Frontier* features its own original universe, civilizations, characters, narrative, gameplay mechanics, visual identity, and systems.

**Silent Frontier is an independent and unofficial project. It is not an authorized adaptation, derivative work, or official extension of The Three-Body Problem series. It has no official affiliation, partnership, or endorsement from Liu Cixin or the relevant rights holders of The Three-Body Problem.**

## 3. Creative Philosophy

*Silent Frontier* explores a fundamental question:

**When a civilization cannot know whether another civilization is hostile, and revealing its existence may lead to its destruction, what does it truly take to survive?**

The game is not simply about maximizing technological progress or military strength.

Instead, it challenges players to make decisions under uncertainty, limited resources, and potentially existential threats.

Technological advancement may create advantages, but it may also increase exposure.

An aggressive strike may eliminate an immediate threat, but it may attract something far more dangerous.

Silence may preserve a civilization, but it may also mean surrendering opportunities that may never return.

In this universe, players must contend not only with their enemies, but also with incomplete information, imperfect judgment, deception, and the long-term consequences of every decision.

## 5. Project Vision

Our ambition is to create a science fiction strategy game that makes players genuinely experience the pressure of civilization survival.

In the vastness of the universe, no civilization can be certain that it possesses the complete truth.

Every signal transmitted may reveal its existence.

Every technological advancement may change its chances of survival.

Every moment of silence may become its final decision.

**Before the universe hears you, decide whether you should send the signal.**
